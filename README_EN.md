## 🧭 What this is (the positioning, up front)

LoveBrain is a **self-growth tool that helps you express yourself sincerely in an intimate relationship — without people-pleasing and without internal friction**.

It gets there through three means:

1. **Options, not scripts** — every message gets 4 reply styles. You pick the one closest to what you'd actually say and make it your own. What gets sent is you, not the AI.
2. **See the relationship clearly** — a relationship vector, a stage tracker and an experience library turn your vague "something feels off" into visible records, reducing anxiety and second-guessing.
3. **Everything stays local** — your relationship data doesn't belong to any company.

The four reply styles are a **means to an end**: they exist so that when you're nervous, flooded, or drawing a blank, you can still turn sincerity into words the other person can actually receive.

## 🚧 Boundaries: what the coach will NOT do

These lines aren't README decoration — they are hard rules **written into the prompt assets themselves** ([engine/system_prompt/redline.md](app/src/main/assets/engine/system_prompt/redline.md), auditable in the open source). This is not a "pickup artist" toolkit:

**Product red lines (enforced at generation time)**

- **No manipulation tactics**: negging, engineered jealousy, cold-shouldering, or diminishing the other person's self-worth are explicitly forbidden in the prompts
- **No invalidating emotions**: suggestions like "you're overthinking" or "you're too sensitive" will never appear
- **No impersonating a human**: the goal of every suggestion is "sounds like something you'd say" — whether to disclose that you used a tool is your honesty call
- **No sending for you**: there is no auto-send. Every message is decided and pressed by you

**User red lines (close this page now if you plan to)**

- ❌ Use it to **harass, stalk, or pressure** anyone who has said no
- ❌ Use any of this on **minors**
- ❌ Use it for **scams, romance fraud, or pig-butchering** — illegal use is on you, and this project offers zero cover for it
- ❌ Resell it as paid "dating coaching" or bulk-reply services — it's MIT open source; don't turn it into an IQ tax

**Expectation management**

- The coach offers **expression suggestions**, not relationship outcomes. It promises no "guaranteed wins"
- Cards are drafts, not scripts: it helps you start, sincerity does the rest
- Real relationship problems (control, contempt, cold violence) can't be fixed by phrasing — as redline.md puts it: when the question becomes "can I still respect myself", the answer is leaving, not talking

This project accepts **no issues and no support** for anything above — and we encourage reporting misuse.

## ✨ What the coach can do

### 🔍 One long-press and the coach takes over

**Long-press her message** in any chat app — the moment the context menu appears, the coach has the context and the floating panel opens. No screenshots, no copy-paste, no switching apps.

### 🃏 One message, four paths

The coach streams **4 reply cards** in different styles, rendered one by one as they're ready:

| Card | What it is |
|---|---|
| 🅰 Steady | The most natural best answer — balanced, low-risk, high tolerance |
| 🅱 Backboned | Has its own stance and boundaries; doesn't people-please |
| 😏 Playful | Catches emotion with humor — cheeky but not sleazy |
| 🫶 Warm | Core vibe: "I'm here" — expresses care without clinging |

Tap one to copy it. You can also tell the coach your thoughts ("don't mention the movie") and the next round will listen.

### 🧠 The more you use it, the better it knows you two

One-shot line generators forget you instantly. This one **stays on the case** — quietly recording and analyzing in the background:

| The coach's brain | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📊 5-dimension vector | Intimacy / Trust / Commitment / Passion / Security — **re-estimated every 3 topics**, with a "why it changed" rationale |
| 🗺️ 8-stage tracker | First contact → Ice-breaking → Ambiguous → In love → Friction → Stable → Crisis → Repair; the AI judges where you are and whether to advance or hold |
| 📓 Experience library | **Every 5 topics**, it distills "lessons specific to you two" (looking back over the last 25) into the knowledge base |
| 🪞 Profiles | Her (her), you (me), and your dynamic (warmth), continuously updated |

The more you talk, the sharper the advice — it knows what stage you're in, what she cares about, and where you fumbled last time.

### 💬 More than flirting — it catches you too

- **Heart-to-heart mode**: she's down, or you had a fight — a six-step framework to unpack the situation and find the words
- **Daily tip**: one stage-appropriate strategy a day — hold steady, move forward, or step back
- **Proactive / Polish**: want to reach out but stuck? Jot a rough draft and get several openers

### 🎈 A persistent bubble

Draggable, edge-snapping, fades when idle and tucks itself half-hidden against the screen edge. A badge pops on the bubble when a new message is captured — there when you need it, invisible when you don't.

### 📚 Your knowledge base, your rules

Multiple knowledge bases (one per person, say), creatable, deletable, switchable:

- **AI-generated starter profile**: answer a questionnaire when creating one, so the coach shows up on day one already briefed
- **Hand-tuning**: a dozen-plus files (profiles, lessons…) are directly editable in-app
- **Import / export**: zip packaging; imports are sanitized, exports come with a plain-text warning

## 🔧 How it works

```
 You long-press her message in any app
         ↓
 The coach captures the message and its context
         ↓
 Assembles the prompt (persona + current stage + profiles + past lessons)
         ↓
 DeepSeek streams the generation → each card appears as it's ready
         ↓
 You pick one and phrase it your own way → the coach logs the round
         ↓
 Background distillation: vector re-estimation / lesson extraction / profile update
         ↓
 Fed back into the next generation — it knows you two better every time
```

In one sentence: a floating-window app + an accessibility service that senses long-presses + a local Markdown knowledge base + the official DeepSeek API. That's all. Full architecture and prompt-assembly details: [docs/ARCHITECTURE.md](docs/ARCHITECTURE.md).

## 🔒 Privacy: strict by design

Item by item, no hand-waving:

- **Zero telemetry, zero upload**: no servers of ours, no analytics SDKs. The only outbound request is the generation call to the official DeepSeek API — that's the product
- **Chat content goes to exactly two places**: the DeepSeek request, and your local knowledge base (plain Markdown in the app's private directory)
- **The accessibility service does two things**: listens for long-press and window-change events to confirm your long-press. No keylogging, no background scanning, no reading your notifications
- **Diagnostics never log content**: the capture pipeline logs event types and text lengths only
- **Panel history dies with the process**: reply-panel messages are memory-only; what persists long-term is distilled conclusions (profiles, lessons), not raw chats
- **API key stored encrypted**: EncryptedSharedPreferences, with an explicit fallback notice on the few devices that don't support it
- **Opted out of cloud backup**: `allowBackup=false` — profiles never ride your account to the cloud
- **Your knowledge base is yours**: local plain-text files, editable, importable/exportable — exports are **plaintext** zips, the app warns you, don't casually upload them
- **No keep-alive tricks**: no persistent notification, no background auto-restart

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Does it read my chat history?**
A: Only at the moment you **long-press a specific message** — it reads that message and its surrounding context, which is the necessary input for suggestions. No background scanning, no keylogging, no notification reading.

**Q: Is my data uploaded anywhere?**
A: The only outbound request is the generation call to the official DeepSeek API when you tap "generate". Everything else stays on-device: the knowledge base is plain Markdown in the app's private directory, cloud backup is disabled, and you can edit, export or delete it anytime.

**Q: Is it free?**
A: The app is open-source and free (MIT). Generation requires your own DeepSeek API key, billed per use by DeepSeek — expect a few RMB per month for casual usage (estimate; see the TODO in Getting Started).

**Q: Is there an iOS version?**
A: Not yet. iOS is far more restrictive around accessibility and floating windows; Android first.

**Q: Will it send messages for me?**
A: No. The coach only suggests — tapping a card copies it. Whether and what you send is always your call.

**Q: DeepSeek only?**
A: The current version is tuned for the official DeepSeek API. Base URL and model name are editable; compatible services may work, but prompts and parameters are DeepSeek-tuned — no guarantees elsewhere.

**Q: Will she know I'm using AI?**
A: The prompts include a naturalness check aiming for human-sounding suggestions. More importantly: treat cards as drafts and rewrite them in your own voice — it opens the door, sincerity keeps it open.
